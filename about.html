Scientific Method

The 4-Step Care Protocol

01

Trichological Scan

We analyze your scalp with 200x magnification to discover root buildup, porosity, and follicle density.

02

Scalp Detox & Steam

Warm ultrasonic ozone mist opens cuticles while salicylic acid gently dissolves product buildup.

03

Deep Peptide & Lipid Infusion

Customized keratin, biomimetic peptides, and argan lipids are pressed deeply into the inner hair cortex.

04

Acidic Gloss & Cosmetic Finishing

An acidic rinse seals cuticles for reflective light shine, complemented by clean cosmetic skin finishing.
