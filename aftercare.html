Hair & Cosmetics Care Guide

Expert trichology rules to extend your keratin smoothing, maintain dimensional color vibrancy, and keep your skin luminous.

Crucial Window

The First 48 Hours: Post-Treatment Protocol

After receiving a keratin nanoplastia, bond repair, or balayage color glaze, your cuticles are locking into place. Follow these essential guidelines:

Keep Hair Strictly Dry

Avoid washing, swimming, or humid saunas for the first 48 hours. If rain or steam touches the hair, immediately blow-dry smooth on low heat.

Prevent Creases & Bends

Do not tie your hair with tight elastics, hair clips, or tuck strands behind your ears during the initial cross-linking period. Wear it down naturally.

Sulfate-Free Shampoos Only

When washing, use exclusively sodium-chloride-free and sulfate-free botanical cleansers to prevent stripping the protective protein shield.

Longevity Routine

Weekly Hair & Scalp Care Routine

Incorporate these trichologist-approved practices to maintain soft, frizz-free hair and vibrant color for months.

Weekly Deep Conditioning Mask

Apply a peptide or lipid mask once weekly. Comb through mid-lengths to ends, wrap in a warm microfiber towel for 15 minutes, then rinse with cool water.

Heat & UV Protection Spray

Thermal styling degrades color molecules and dries keratin. Always apply a botanical leave-in thermal mist (up to 450°F protection) before blow-drying.

Sleep on Pure Mulberry Silk

Cotton pillowcases create friction that frays hair cuticles and absorbs scalp moisture. Silk pillowcases preserve your blowout and stop morning bedhead.

Cosmetics Mastery

Clean Cosmetics Hygiene & Skin Prep

To maintain a glowing complexion and protect your skin barrier while wearing cosmetics, follow these dermatologist-guided habits:

01

Clean Brushes Weekly

Cosmetic brushes accumulate skin oils and bacteria. Clean synthetic brushes with tea tree antibacterial wash to avoid breakout flare-ups.

02

Double Cleanse Routine

Break down mineral foundation and eye cosmetics first with a botanical melting balm, followed by a gentle pH 5.5 hydrating foam wash.

03

Hydrate Before Priming

Never apply cosmetics onto dehydrated skin. Layer a hyaluronic serum and light squalane moisturizer 5 minutes before applying foundation.

04

Check Expirations

Clean, preservative-free cosmetics should be refreshed regularly. Replace organic mascaras every 3 months and liquid foundations annually.
